How To Reset Browser Notification Permission

Google Chrome: Click the Lock/Info icon to the left of the URL in the address bar and toggle the Notifications switch, or click Reset permission.

Firefox: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Permissions, then click the Settings button next to "Notifications".

Safari: Go to Settings > Websites > Notifications and remove the specific site from the list.
